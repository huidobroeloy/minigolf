# Code Lyoko lore bible

The canon reference for Lyoko Minigolf. Every design change that claims to be "from the show" is checked against
this page. Each fact names its source; episode numbers follow the **French guide numbering** used by
codelyoko.fr and codelyoko-leguide.fr (English Wikipedia's "overall" numbers differ by a few episodes).

Researched November 2026. The show is 95 episodes over four seasons plus the two-part prequel "XANA's
Awakening", 2003–2007. *Code Lyoko Évolution* (2013) is **not canon** per the creators; a season 5 was announced as
being written in June 2026 [FR-WIKI].

## Lyoko
- **Five territories.** Four surface sectors (Forest, Mountain, Ice, Desert), each with **10 towers**, plus Sector 5
  (Carthage) at the centre with one more tower: 41 in all [TOURS]. Lyoko floats in the **digital void**; below it is
  the **Digital Sea** [VIDNUM].
- **Surface monsters:** Kankrelats, Bloks, Krabes, Hornets, Megatanks, and Tarantulas from season 2 [FR-WIKI].
  **Guardians** only appear in the Ice and Mountain sectors (season 1, eps 7 and 10) [FR-WIKI].
- **Towers:** blue when neutral or used by Aelita, **red when XANA activates one**, green for Jérémie, white for
  Franz Hopper; from ep 52 every neutral tower is white [TOURS]. A tower with no halo is damaged (Megatank fire or
  cut cables). Classic towers pass you along a one-way data flow; each sector's **passage tower** leads to the next
  sector [TOURS]. Only Aelita can deactivate a tower, by typing "Code Lyoko" at its interface [EN-WIKI, TOURS].
- **Sector 5 / Carthage** [CARTHAGE]:
  - It's "a giant ball inside a giant sphere": the Core ball floats in a hollow sphere that touches the Digital Sea,
    and four data flows link it to the four sectors.
  - Getting there: no passage tower. The **Transporter** (password SCIPIO) collects the heroes at the edge of a
    sector and drops them in the **Arena**, a round hall with a big XANA eye on the floor.
  - Leaving the Arena starts a **countdown**; the **labyrinth** of rooms reconfigures every visit and turns deadly if
    the countdown runs out. **Creepers** guard it.
  - The **Key** (a XANA eye; its pupil is the trigger) stops the countdown and opens the way. Key rooms have traps:
    falling blocks (ep 34), a moving-floor puzzle (ep 48), rising blocks (ep 65).
  - Then a fast **circular elevator** that never stops (you jump on), up to the **Celestial Dome**, where a path
    over the void leads to the interface. Touching the interface makes XANA hatch **Mantas** from the vault.
  - The **Core room** is entered through huge snapping jaws: "ending up between its fangs means devirtualization".
    The **Core of Lyoko** is a yellow sphere ringed by four XANA eyes, behind self-regenerating cube barriers.
  - From season 4, the **Skid garage** is in north Carthage: a lift, four pillars and five boarding pads (one for
    Aelita, four for the Navskids).
- **The Digital Sea** [VIDNUM]: the part of the global network that seeped into Lyoko. A hero who falls in is
  "digitized forever", crushed and scattered or swallowed by the data flow. Recovery means finding them, then a
  materialization program. Yumi was saved in ep 6 at the cost of Aelita's only copy of it. After ep 25 Jérémie masters
  the program, but a fall "stays strongly inadvisable" and the odds are "infinitesimal". The Kolossus can walk in it
  (ep 93).
- **Replikas** [REPLIKA]: XANA's copies of Lyoko, "islands drifting in an infinite ocean" (the global network), each
  one territory with usable towers, discovered in eps 71 and 73. **The Volcano territory only exists in the Wii video
  game**, not the series. Known Replikas: copies of the surface sectors (a Forest Replika in ep 78, an Ice one) and a
  Carthage Replika (ep 87). The heroes destroy them with the ATA program.
- **The Skidbladnir** (season 4): Aelita's submarine, with four **Navskids** clamped to it for close combat in the
  Digital Sea [S4]. The Kolossus destroys it in ep 93 [S4].
- **The Cortex, Tyron's ninjas** and the **Megapod** come from *Évolution* (non-canon) [NINJAS, FR-WIKI].

## The sectors, for level design
- **Desert** [DESERT]: dry, yellow, sun at its zenith, no greenery. The largest sector: **huge plateaus joined only by
  small bridges**. **Canyons** wind a long way (Megatanks run you over in them); **pits**: some bottomless, straight
  into the Digital Sea, some with a floor and a tower at the bottom, reached on foot by a **spiral path**. Giant rocks
  and **long flat rocks planted upright or at an angle** give cover. Kankrelats hide behind the relief; Megatanks and
  Tarantulas use the open ground; Hornets the big open spaces; Bloks almost never. A sandstorm in ep 4. The Desert
  Replika has a canyon trench dotted with rock columns (ep 83).
- **Forest** [FORET]: humid, tropical light through dense leaves (bright green from season 2). **Many winding paths**
  that split and rejoin at medium platforms (often with a tower); paths are sometimes cut so you must jump; **fallen
  logs** join paths and **hollow stumps** hold tunnels (ep 16); trees float over the Digital Sea. Dark **ponds** by
  towers, reached over log bridges. Krabes are very common (they run the paths), Kankrelats come from several paths at
  once, Hornets crash into trees. The Marabounta invaded it (ep 36).
- **Mountain** [MONTAGNE]: cold, permanent thin **mist**, bonsai trees, a slightly East-Asian mood. **Long bridges**
  between medium plateaus, rocks of every size, narrow passages between rocks, a maze (ep 15), **floating
  mountains**, hollow mountains with tunnels and caves (no mist inside). Its signature is **moving platforms**: a tower
  on a **rotating island** reached by jumping onto a turning arm (ep 20), and platforms that **rise and fall** then
  **circle round the tower**. Thick fog hides monsters and lifts when the tower is deactivated. Hornets, Mantas and Bloks
  (they climb the peaks) are at home; Megatanks block the narrow paths.
- **Ice** [BANQUISE]: night, −15 °C, a thin layer of snow on eternal ice: **slippery** for everyone. **Ice plateaus**
  joined by long frozen paths, icebergs with **caves, waterfalls, rivers and tunnels**; tunnels are long slides that
  often open onto a **cliff**. The ice **cracks and breaks** into floating floes (ep 38). **Lakes at path height**
  (monsters sink). Towers hide in **caves behind waterfalls guarded by Tarantulas**. Hornet squadrons; Bloks hide under
  the ice (ep 31); Guardians (eps 7, 10).
- **The Network** [RESEAU]: an ocean "with no bottom and no surface", calm transparent blue that turns red when XANA
  lurks, giant red data blocks with white graffiti, and **hubs** that shoot you across at hyperspeed. Sharks, Kongres,
  the Kalamar (ep 88 only), William's Rorkal (ep 77).

## Life points and devirtualization
- No life-point number is given for the heroes in the series. codelyoko.fr's estimates imply 100 (a Kankrelat's
  point-blank shot cost Odd "half his capital", 50, ep 22) [PROFILS].
- At 0 LP a hero is **devirtualized** and rematerializes in the Factory's scanners, almost automatically [VIDNUM].
  In Carthage a hero who loses all LP isn't rematerialized until the right program is run [CARTHAGE].
- Instant devirtualizations: the Megatank's beam can take every LP [PROFILS]; a Blok's fire ring "devirtualizes in
  one shot" (ep 59) [PROFILS]; a Manta mine devirtualized Ulrich (ep 66) [PROFILS]; the Kolossus devirtualizes in
  one hit [EN-WIKI]; William's zanbato "devirtualizes in one hit" [S4]; the Core room's jaws [CARTHAGE].
- **Return to the Past**: after a tower is deactivated, Jérémie sends the world back in time; everyone scanned keeps
  their memory [EN-WIKI]. Using it over and over **makes XANA stronger** (ep 30) [EN-EPS].
- Virtualization call: "Transfer Ulrich… Scanner Ulrich… Virtualization!" [VIRTMAT].

**The Marabounta** (ep 36) [GUIDE36]:
- **What it is:** not XANA's: it's **Jérémie's own multi-agent program**, built from Franz Hopper's journal, "an
  assembly of small dark shape-shifting spheres" made to hunt XANA's monsters.
- **What it does:** it spreads over the Forest sector, "destroys XANA's monsters without discrimination", cuts the
  heroes off from the tower, and then turns on **Aelita**, because XANA's virus in her makes it see her as a monster.
  Yumi is engulfed.
- **How it ends:**
  - Jérémie puts a counter-program into one of Odd's laser arrows. It must hit the **mother cell**, which floats
    about a metre off the ground, guarded by its offspring.
  - Odd rides a **Krabe** through the tide of spheres to reach it: XANA's monsters help, as it's the first alliance
    between XANA and the heroes.
  - The blast destroys every sphere.

**XANA** [EN-WIKI, TOURS]:
- **What it is:** a multi-agent program in the supercomputer. It activates towers to reach the real world.
- **Its powers:**
  - possessing machines, elements and people (they carry its eye);
  - **specters** that come out of power sockets;
  - **polymorphic clones**;
  - in ep 30, taking over the Return to the Past.
- **Possessed William:** he can type the XANA code to take a Replika tower (ep 78).
